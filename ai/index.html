What we plan to build next

Our Claude API roadmap.

Planned · Not yet released

We plan to integrate Anthropic’s Claude API into Cuisy and evaluate it for the cooking tasks below. The Claude integration is not currently part of the released app.

Clearer recipe understanding

Explore turning extracted recipe text into consistent ingredients, quantities, and ordered cooking steps.

Answers from your cookbooks

Explore answering questions using relevant passages retrieved from a user’s own uploaded PDF collection, with source references.

Ideas around your ingredients

Explore recipe suggestions and substitutions based on the ingredients and preferences a user provides.

This is a development plan, not an available feature or a release-date commitment. Cuisy is independently developed; mentioning Claude does not imply endorsement, partnership, or acceptance into an Anthropic program.

Keeping the cook in control

Helpful suggestions.

Review before you cook.

AI can misread a quantity or miss a step. Check ingredients, allergens, and food-safety instructions before using a recipe.

Your books stay your collection.

Cookbook search is scoped to your account. Processing uses service providers described in our Privacy Policy.

Evaluate before release.

For the planned Claude integration, we intend to compare extraction accuracy, source references, response time, and cost before deciding what to ship.

Our Privacy Policy describes current processing. We will update relevant disclosures before introducing a new AI provider into the released app.
