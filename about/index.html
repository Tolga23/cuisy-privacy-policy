The idea behind Cuisy

More from your kitchen.
Less in your way.

Cuisy brings your recipes, cookbooks, and ingredients together, helping you turn everyday inspiration into meals you can make.

A familiar problem

Inspiration is everywhere.
Dinner still takes work.

A recipe saved in a video app. A favorite buried in a cookbook. Ingredients in the fridge waiting for an idea. Cuisy brings these separate pieces into one cooking workflow.

The aim is practical: help you keep a recipe, understand what you need, and follow it through to a meal. AI supports that journey through recipe capture, cookbook search, and new ideas.

Cuisy is available on Android, with a free plan for everyday cooking and an optional Premium subscription for more AI, import, and sync features.

What we’re building toward

A cooking companion for the whole journey.

Our ambition is to connect the recipe that inspires you with the week you plan, the food you buy, and the meals you track. Today, Cuisy brings recipes, ingredients, shopping lists, and cooking steps together.

Next, we plan weekly meal planning, calorie & macro tracking, and grocery integrations tailored to each supported country, alongside ideas for coordinated shopping and using leftovers. These future features will need prototyping, data integration, and testing before release.
