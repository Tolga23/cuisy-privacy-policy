Quick FAQ

Short answers for the most common support questions.

How do I create an account? +

Register using email/password, Google Sign-In, or Apple Sign-In in the app onboarding/auth screens.

Can I use Cuisy without premium? +

Yes. Free tier is offline-first and keeps core recipe, fridge, and shopping data on your device.

How does premium change storage? +

Premium adds optional cloud sync for cross-device usage and broader AI feature access.

Are uploaded PDF cookbooks private? +

Yes. Cookbook indexing and search are user-scoped. Other users cannot search or access your uploaded cookbook library.

Why can a parse job fail? +

Typical causes: unsupported links, low image quality, temporary network issues, or usage quota limits.

How do I delete my account if I cannot log in? +

Send a deletion request to tolgadev23@gmail.com from your registered email address.
