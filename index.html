FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still stuck? Email support@getcuisy.com or visit Support.

Can I use Cuisy without Premium?

Yes. The free plan includes your recipe library, manual fridge tracking, shopping lists, Cook Mode and 3 video imports per month. Saved essentials work offline; imports and AI features need an internet connection.

What does Premium add?

Optional cloud sync across devices, a higher video import allowance, PDF cookbook search, camera ingredient scans and AI recipe ideas. Current pricing and availability are shown in the app.

Is Cuisy available on iPhone?

Yes. Cuisy is available on iPhone from the App Store and on Android from Google Play.

Are my uploaded PDF cookbooks private?

Your cookbook library is scoped to your account. Other users can’t browse or search your uploaded books. Processing may involve service providers, as described in the Privacy Policy.

Why did my recipe import fail?

An unsupported link, a low-quality source, a temporary network issue or a usage limit can interrupt an import. Check your connection and allowance, then try again. If it keeps happening, email us the link and any error message.

How do I delete my account or request my data?

Start account deletion in the app. If you can’t access the app, or want a copy of your data, email privacy@getcuisy.com from your registered address. Read the full deletion instructions.
