Recipe app features.
Fewer moving parts.

Turn a cooking video into a recipe, work with what you have, and make something you’ll want to cook again. All in one companion for your kitchen.

01 / Video to recipe

Recipes beyond
your saved videos.

Turn a supported YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram cooking video into a recipe you can return to.

  • Keep ingredients and steps in one place.
  • Browse and search your recipe library.
  • Get 3 video imports per month on Free, with a higher allowance on Premium.
How video to recipe works ↗
02 / Rediscover your collection

Your books.
New possibilities.

Bring PDF cookbooks into Cuisy with Premium. Search your own books with AI to find recipes for the ingredients you select.

  • Save extracted recipes to your library.
  • Search is limited to books uploaded to your account.
  • Other users cannot browse or search your uploaded collection.
03 / Cook with what you have

Open the fridge.
Find your next meal.

Add ingredients, match them to your saved recipes, and see what’s missing before you head to the grocery store.

  • Manual fridge tracking and basic matching are included free.
  • Add missing ingredients to your shopping list.
  • Premium adds camera ingredient scans and AI recipe ideas.
Recipe library and grocery list ↗
04 / Enjoy the making

One step.
Then the next.

Cook Mode keeps the screen awake and shows one step at a time, so you can focus on the food.

  • Listen to instructions read aloud.
  • Run multiple cooking timers.
  • On supported devices, use voice commands to move through your recipe.
