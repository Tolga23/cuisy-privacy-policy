01 / Save the inspiration

Great recipes.
Beyond your saved videos.

Turn a supported YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram cooking video into a recipe you can come back to.

  • Keep ingredients and instructions together.
  • Build and search your personal recipe library.
  • Start free with 3 video imports per month. Premium gives you a higher allowance.
02 / Rediscover your books Premium

Your own cookbooks.
A fresh way to explore.

Import PDF cookbooks and use AI search to find recipes for your selected ingredients. Save the ones you want to make to your collection.

Search is limited to the books in your account. Other users cannot browse or search your uploaded library.

03 / Make the most of what’s there

Open the fridge.
Find your next meal.

Add ingredients, match them to saved recipes, and see what you’re missing before you start.

  • Manual fridge tracking and basic matching are included free.
  • Add missing ingredients to your shopping list.
  • With Premium, scan ingredients with your camera or ask AI for new recipe ideas.
04 / Enjoy the making

Less tapping.
More tasting.

Cook Mode keeps your screen awake, gives each step room to breathe, and helps you keep track of the timing.

  • Listen to instructions read aloud.
  • Run multiple cooking timers.
  • On supported devices, use voice commands to move through your recipe.
