Video to recipe

Turn a cooking video
into a recipe you can cook.

Cooking videos are great for inspiration and hard to cook from. Cuisy turns a TikTok, Instagram or YouTube cooking video into a written recipe with ingredients and steps, so you can stop pausing and rewinding and start cooking.

Coming soon on the App Store
What you get

The recipe,
written down.

Cuisy works from what the creator shares: what’s said in the video, text shown on screen, and the post’s caption, where many creators list their ingredients.

  • A recipe title, taken from the video when the dish is named.
  • Ingredients with amounts and units.
  • Cooking steps, in order.
  • Servings and prep and cook times, when the video gives them.
  • A link back to the original video, so you can watch it again from the recipe.
How it works

From link to recipe
in four steps.

Importing needs an internet connection. Once saved, the recipe stays in your library on your device.

  1. Copy the video’s link

    In TikTok, Instagram or YouTube, tap Share on the video and choose Copy link. If Cuisy appears in your phone’s share menu, you can share the video to Cuisy directly instead.

  2. Paste it into Cuisy

    Open Cuisy, tap the chef button in the middle of the tab bar and choose Video. Paste the link (if you just copied it, Cuisy can paste it for you) and tap Extract.

  3. Let Cuisy write it out

    The video is processed in the background, so you can keep using the app. With notifications on, Cuisy lets you know when the recipe is ready.

  4. Check it, then cook

    Compare the amounts and steps with the video before you start: AI can misread a quantity or miss a step. Then adjust servings, add missing ingredients to your shopping list, or open Cook Mode.

Video to recipe questions.

Which apps can I turn cooking videos into recipes from?

TikTok, Instagram (Reels and video posts) and YouTube (videos and Shorts). Copy the link to the video and paste it into Cuisy.

What if the video doesn’t give the amounts?

Cuisy can only write down what the video, its on-screen text or its caption actually contain. If an amount is missing or unclear, check the original video and adjust as you cook.

Is turning videos into recipes free?

Yes. Cuisy Free includes 3 video imports each month. Premium raises the allowance, and the current limits are shown in the app.

Do I need an internet connection?

Importing a video needs a connection. Once a recipe is saved, you can open it, match it to your fridge and cook from it offline.

Why did my import fail?

An unsupported or private link, a video without a recipe, a temporary network issue, or a reached limit can stop an import. Check the link and your connection, then try again. If it keeps happening, email support@getcuisy.com with the link.
