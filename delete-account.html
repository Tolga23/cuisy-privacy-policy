Delete your Cuisy account

This page explains how to delete your account in the Cuisy app (Android and iOS), how to ask us to delete it if you can no longer use the app, which data is deleted, and which data is kept and for how long.

Delete your account in the app

The fastest way. Deletion takes effect immediately.

  1. Open Cuisy and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile picture (or initials) in the top-left corner of the Kitchen, Library or Lists tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of your profile and tap Delete Account.
  4. Review what will be deleted and tap Continue to Delete.
  5. Type DELETE and tap Delete Account. If you signed in with Apple, confirm with Apple when asked.

Have a subscription? Deleting your account does not cancel a Cuisy Premium subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play (Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions) or on iPhone in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Can't use the app? Ask us by email

If you uninstalled Cuisy or can't sign in, we delete your account for you.

  1. Email tolgadev23@gmail.com with the subject Cuisy account deletion request.
  2. Send it from the email address of your Google or Apple account that you use to sign in to Cuisy, so we can verify that the account is yours.
  3. We confirm by email and delete the account and the data listed below within 30 days.

What is deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove from our servers:

  • Your account: name, email address, sign-in identifiers and push notification token.
  • Your recipes, including ingredients, steps, tags and recipe images.
  • Your fridge items and shopping list.
  • Your recipe imports and AI job history (video links, scans and AI suggestions).
  • Your uploaded PDF cookbooks and their search index.
  • Your photos and uploaded files in our storage. These are removed by a background task shortly after the deletion and retried until they are gone.
  • Your subscription status stored by Cuisy.

The app also clears your Cuisy data from the phone it was deleted on. If you signed in with Apple, Cuisy is removed from your Apple ID.

What is kept, and for how long

DataWhyHow long
A deletion receipt: a random ID, the time of deletion and a scrambled code. It holds no name, email or content. To confirm to the app that the deletion finished. Kept; it cannot be linked back to you.
Subscription purchase events received from our payment provider (product, dates, store transaction IDs). Billing records, refunds and fraud prevention. Up to 30 days, unless the law requires longer.
Purchase and order history held by Google Play, Apple and RevenueCat. Held by those companies under their own policies. Under the provider's own retention policy.
Crash reports (Sentry). They are never linked to your account. Fixing app crashes. Deleted automatically within 90 days.
Server backups that still contain your data. Disaster recovery only. We never restore deleted accounts from them. Overwritten within 30 days.

Delete some data without deleting your account

  • Recipes: open a recipe in your Library, tap the … menu and delete it.
  • Fridge items: in the Kitchen tab, tap Edit, select items and delete them.
  • Shopping list: swipe an item in the Lists tab to delete it.

If you use Premium cloud sync, deleted items are also removed from our servers on the next sync. To have other data deleted while you keep your account, email tolgadev23@gmail.com from your sign-in email address and tell us what to delete. We handle the request within 30 days.
