# Privacy Policy for Cuisy

**Last Updated:** February 10, 2026  
**Effective Date:** February 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cuisy collects, uses, stores, and shares personal data when you use the Cuisy mobile application and related services.

Cuisy is an offline-first recipe management app that supports:
- Manual recipe management
- Video URL to recipe parsing
- PDF cookbook upload and AI search
- Meal and fridge photo analysis
- Fridge and shopping list management
- Optional premium cloud sync

## 3. Data We Collect

### 3.1 Account and Identity Data
When you create or use an account, we may collect:
- Email address
- Display name
- Authentication provider (email/password, Google, Apple)
- Authentication identifiers and tokens needed to keep you signed in securely

### 3.2 Recipe and Content Data
We process content you create or upload, including:
- Recipes, ingredients, steps, notes, ratings, tags, favorites, cooking history
- Video links submitted for parsing (YouTube and other supported platforms)
- PDF cookbooks uploaded for indexing/search
- Meal photos and fridge photos uploaded for AI analysis
- Source metadata (for example source URL and citation/page references)

### 3.3 Kitchen Management Data
- Fridge items (name, category, quantity, unit, expiration, low stock, notes)
- Shopping list items (name, category, quantity, unit, status, notes, order)
- Ingredient matching results (matched and missing ingredients)

### 3.4 Preferences and Settings
- Unit system (metric/imperial)
- Theme and haptics preferences
- Dietary and meal preferences
- Notification preferences

### 3.5 Subscription and Transaction Metadata
For premium subscriptions:
- Subscription status, tier, store, renewal/expiry dates
- RevenueCat app user identifiers and entitlement metadata

We do **not** receive full payment card details from Apple or Google.

### 3.6 Technical and Usage Data
We may process:
- App version, device model, OS version
- Push notification token
- Feature usage events, quota counters, and job status/error logs
- Basic diagnostics and crash/performance telemetry (if enabled in the app/configuration)

### 3.7 Data We Do Not Intentionally Collect
Cuisy does not require:
- Precise GPS location
- Contacts, call logs, SMS
- Microphone recordings for core functionality

## 4. Permissions We Request

Cuisy requests permissions only when needed by a feature:
- **Camera/Photos:** meal photo capture, fridge scan, profile image
- **Files/Documents:** selecting PDF cookbooks for upload
- **Notifications:** recipe/capture/job and account related alerts (optional)

If you deny a permission, some features may be limited, but core local recipe management remains available.

## 5. How We Use Data

We use your data to:
- Create and secure your account
- Provide recipe, fridge, and shopping list functionality
- Run AI capture and search flows you initiate
- Enforce usage quotas and subscription entitlements
- Sync data across devices for premium users (optional feature)
- Send requested notifications
- Detect abuse, protect security, and debug service issues
- Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal data.

---

## 6. Free Tier vs Premium Tier Processing

### Free Tier (Offline-First)
- Data is primarily stored locally on your device (SQLite)
- Core app features work without cloud sync

### Premium Tier (Optional Cloud Sync)
- Your data is mirrored to backend services for cross-device sync and premium AI features
- Sync is asynchronous and designed not to block app interaction

## 9. Data Retention

Retention depends on account type and feature usage:

- **On-device data (free and premium):** kept until you delete it, delete your account, or uninstall the app
- **Premium synced cloud data:** kept while your account is active
- **Capture jobs and operational logs:** retained only as needed for service delivery, fraud prevention, security, and legal obligations

When you request account deletion:
- Data is removed from active systems without undue delay
- We target completion within **30 days**
- Residual encrypted backups are overwritten on a rolling schedule

Where law requires longer retention, we keep only the minimum required data for the required period.
