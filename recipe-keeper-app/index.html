Recipe keeper app

A recipe keeper
that helps you cook.

Cuisy keeps the recipes you save from cooking videos and cookbooks in one searchable library, then helps you cook them: with what’s in your fridge, a grocery list for what isn’t, and step-by-step Cook Mode.

Choosing a recipe organizer

What to look for
in a recipe keeper app.

A few questions worth asking before you move your recipes in, and how Cuisy answers them.

Does it save recipes from where you find them?

A lot of recipe inspiration now comes from short cooking videos. Cuisy turns TikTok, Instagram and YouTube videos into written recipes.

Does it work in the kitchen, offline?

Your saved recipes, fridge and shopping list stay on your device and work without a connection.

Does it scale servings and units?

Change the servings and the amounts follow, in metric or imperial.

Does it connect recipes to your grocery list?

Ingredients you don’t have go to your shopping list; the ones already in your fridge stay off it.

Does it make your cookbooks useful?

With Premium, search your own PDF cookbooks for recipes that use the ingredients you choose. Search stays within the books you uploaded.

Can you leave whenever you want?

You can delete your account in the app. Read what is deleted and what is kept.

Recipe keeper questions.

Is Cuisy a free recipe keeper?

Yes. Cuisy Free includes your recipe library, 3 video imports a month, manual fridge tracking and recipe matching, shopping lists and Cook Mode. Premium is optional.

Can I use Cuisy on more than one device?

Premium adds cross-device sync. On the free plan, your recipe library is stored on your device.

Does Cuisy have a grocery list?

Yes. Add the ingredients a recipe is missing to your shopping list, then tick them off as you shop. The list works offline.

Is Cuisy available on iPhone?

Cuisy is available on Android through Google Play today. The iPhone app is coming soon to the App Store.
